About Me

I’m Saksham Singh Sachan, a Computer Science engineering student with a strong interest in cybersecurity, ethical hacking, software development, and security research. I enjoy learning through hands-on challenges and documenting what I discover along the way. My cybersecurity journey includes CTFs, OverTheWire, PortSwigger Web Security Academy, TryHackMe, bug bounty hunting, penetration testing, and security tools. Alongside cybersecurity, I’m also working on some ambitious personal projects, including developing my own operating system and designing my own programming language. These projects allow me to explore systems programming, computer architecture, compilers, operating systems, and low-level computing. Through SSSWriteups, I document my learning, experiments, methodologies, commands, and lessons learned, while building a structured knowledge base that can also help other people learning cybersecurity. My goal is to continuously improve my technical skills, explore cybersecurity and low-level computing, build things from the ground up, and eventually contribute meaningful work to the technology and security community.
